Crew Teacher 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 8 日

概述

Crew Teacher 是用於口說與語言練習的 Android 應用程式。本政策說明 App 在您使用即時 AI 語音對話、字幕與學習功能時如何處理資料。

我們不販售個人資料，也未整合廣告 SDK 或自有雲端資料庫。

我們處理的資料

麥克風音訊與逐字稿

您主動開始語音練習時，App 會使用麥克風，並透過 TLS/WSS 加密連線將即時音訊及對話內容傳送至 Google Gemini API，以提供語音對話、輸入轉寫、字幕與回饋。App 不會將原始錄音檔保存到裝置或我們的伺服器。

學習資料與偏好

API Key、語言與音色偏好、對話逐字稿、學習活動與課後報告會保存在 App 的私有 Android SharedPreferences 儲存空間中，以提供歷史紀錄與個人化學習功能。這些資料不會傳送至 Crew Teacher 自有伺服器。

API Key

您提供的 Gemini API Key 僅用於由您的裝置直接連線至 Google Gemini API；我們不會收集或存放該 Key 於自有伺服器。

權限

  • 麥克風：用於您主動開始的即時口說練習。
  • 前景麥克風服務：在語音對話進行中維持可見通知，讓您切換 App 時仍可繼續練習。
  • 顯示於其他 App 上層：選用的桌面懸浮對話泡泡；可隨時在系統設定撤銷。

App 不使用無障礙服務、相機或位置權限。

第三方服務

Google Gemini API 會依 Google 的條款與隱私政策處理您傳送的資料。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策 與 Gemini API 服務條款。

資料控制與聯絡方式

您可在 App 內結束對話、刪除相關紀錄，或在 Android 系統設定中清除 App 資料／解除安裝，以刪除本機保存的資料與設定。您也可隨時撤銷 App 權限。

若有隱私問題，請至 Crew Teacher GitHub Issues 聯絡我們。

Overview

Crew Teacher is an Android language-learning app for speaking practice. This policy explains how data is handled when you use live AI voice conversations, subtitles, and learning features.

We do not sell personal data and do not use advertising SDKs or our own cloud database.

Data We Process

Microphone audio and transcripts

When you actively start a voice session, the App uses your microphone and sends real-time audio and conversation content to the Google Gemini API over TLS/WSS to provide voice conversation, transcription, subtitles, and feedback. The App does not save raw audio recordings on your device or our servers.

Learning data and preferences

Your API key, language and voice preferences, conversation transcripts, learning activity, and post-session reports are stored in the App's private Android SharedPreferences storage to provide history and personalized learning. They are not sent to Crew Teacher-operated servers.

API key

Your Gemini API key is used only for direct requests from your device to the Google Gemini API. We do not collect or store it on our servers.

Permissions

  • Microphone: for live speaking practice that you start.
  • Microphone foreground service: to keep an ongoing voice session visible through a notification while you use other apps.
  • Display over other apps: optional floating conversation bubble, which you may revoke in Android settings.

The App does not use Accessibility Service, camera, or location permissions.

Third-Party Services

Google Gemini API processes data sent to it under Google's terms and privacy policy. See the Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms.

Your Controls and Contact

You may end a session, delete related records in the App, or clear App data/uninstall it in Android settings to remove locally stored data and settings. You may revoke permissions at any time.

For privacy questions, contact us through Crew Teacher GitHub Issues.
